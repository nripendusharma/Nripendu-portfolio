Product Philosophy

I build products where failure is expensive and users did not ask for software. For eight years I have worked in GovTech and enterprise HRTech: sectors with high user resistance, heavy compliance, and a cost of failure measured in millions of dollars and public trust. My job, as I see it, is translating fragmented field workflows and ambiguous mandates into scalable, auditable systems of record.

I work rules-first, evidence-first. At UPPCL I shipped deterministic anomaly detection before any model, then earned the way to AI in phase 2 by building the closed-loop evaluation system that could measure it. I have run discovery with field officers in low-connectivity regions, written acceptance criteria for a twelve-engineer remote team, and defended every roadmap decision to IAS-level leadership.

My current conviction: for AI products, specifying behavior is the product work. I write the behavior spec and the evals before the feature, and I am currently proving that in public with Voyogo, my AI-native travel diary, where the eval harness was committed before the first prompt.

Deep Dive: UPPCL Raid Management

01. The Problem Space

Operational Chaos & Revenue Leakage

Before this initiative, the Uttar Pradesh Power Corporation Limited (UPPCL) faced a critical revenue hemorrhage. Enforcement officers, the frontline workers tasked with detecting electricity theft, were operating without structured data parameters. Inspections were manual, fragmented, and driven by intuition rather than telemetry. Because there was no verifiable audit trail, corruption and inefficiencies thrived, and bill recovery workflows remained heavily bottlenecked.

02. The Solution

AI-Assisted Operational Intelligence

Led the strategic transformation from static ledger systems to a proactive, data-driven Raid Management platform. Spearheaded the MVP for low-tech-literacy field officers, scaled to 35K+ across 20 districts.

MY ROLE

Solo PM owning strategy through rollout: 12 engineers, 2 designers, an external vendor under my acceptance criteria, reporting to IAS-level leadership. Every PRD, behavior spec, and release decision on this platform was mine.

  • ✓ Consumption Anomaly Alerts: Replaced random field checks with targeted raids based on historical meter data anomalies, utilizing logic to flag potential theft locations with high accuracy before officers even arrived.
  • ✓ Irrefutable Audit Trails: Integrated secure geolocation tagging and automated report generation, eliminating discrepancies, falsified records, and manual paperwork on-site.
  • ✓ Offline-First Architecture: Ensuring the application functioned flawlessly in remote districts without internet access, syncing data asynchronously once connectivity was restored to maintain continuous operations.

03. Feature Insight

Target Profile Analysis Dashboard

Phase 1 shipped deterministic anomaly rules, not AI: consumption baselines, sanctioned-load vs. actual-pattern deltas, and geolocation signals. AI came in phase 2, once a closed-loop evaluation system existed to measure it, lifting detection precision to 23%. The critical challenge in both phases was translating system outputs into actionable directives for non-technical field officers. Designed a centralized "Raid Intelligence" dashboard that correlated sanctioned load vs. actual transformer patterns to accurately predict bypasses and dispatch officers.

Key Decisions & Tradeoffs

What I chose, and what it cost
Rules before AI

Decision: Ship deterministic anomaly rules in phase 1; defer AI until a closed-loop evaluation system existed to measure it.

Tradeoff accepted: Lower phase-1 detection ceiling and a less impressive demo, in exchange for auditability, officer trust, and a measurable baseline AI had to beat.

Transparency over speed

Decision: Architect audit trails and traceable model-assisted actions into the core, meeting anti-corruption policy from day one.

Tradeoff accepted: Slower initial build and heavier data model, in exchange for anti-corruption compliance from day one and the officer trust the platform ran on.

Vendor as embedded team

Decision: Run the external vendor on my sprint cadence, SLAs, and acceptance criteria instead of milestone-based delivery.

Tradeoff accepted: More of my week spent on delivery management, in exchange for predictable iterative AI releases instead of big-bang drops.

Deep Dive: Enterprise Candidate UX Redesign

01. The Problem Space

Security vs. Candidate Empathy

Talview provides sophisticated AI-powered hiring and proctoring solutions to global enterprises. This platform demands rigorous security measures including browser lockdown, multi-camera monitoring, and identity verification. However, these vital security protocols created an intimidating wall of friction for candidates. Users were bombarded with aggressive hardware permission requests with zero context, causing massive abandonment rates that directly damaged the employer brands of major enterprise clients, including Cisco and Nvidia.

MY ROLE

PM owning Candidate Experience end to end for enterprise accounts: discovery, funnel instrumentation, redesign spec, A/B program across 11 cohorts, and the phased enterprise GTM.

02. Strategy & Discovery

The Data-Driven Friction Audit

Instead of relying on anecdotal complaints, systematic tracking of the entire candidate activation funnel was instrumented in Mixpanel. Tracking every micro-interaction isolated the exact moments of highest decay. Funnel metrics showed that 65% of all drop-offs occurred precisely at the "System Hardware Check" screen. Armed with this behavioral data, a prioritized roadmap utilized an Impact vs. Effort matrix to deliver contextual nudges and asynchronous background checks over complex UI overhauls.

03. Feature Insight

Onboarding Funnel Optimization

The redesigned flow replaced aggressive blocking prompts with a phased, empathetic "workspace setup" flow that explained the necessity behind every camera and screen-share request.

Key Decisions & Tradeoffs

What I chose, and what it cost
Security vs. experience is a false binary

Decision: Keep every proctoring control, but move permission requests to the moment of relevance with plain-language context.

Tradeoff accepted: Longer redesign cycle with the security team at the table, instead of the quick fix of just softening copy.

Instrument before redesigning

Decision: Spend the first weeks wiring funnel analytics to find the true drop-off points rather than trusting stakeholder intuition.

Tradeoff accepted: Visible delay before any UI shipped, in exchange for aiming the redesign at the 65% hardware-check cliff instead of guesses.

Phased GTM over big-bang

Decision: Roll out by enterprise cohort with enablement, validating each before the next.

Tradeoff accepted: Slower headline launch, in exchange for zero enterprise escalations during migration of 100K+ candidates.

Deep Dive: DMS Turnaround & Tender Qualification

01. The Problem Space

Feature Bloat & Rapid Churn

Magnus IT Solutions provided a critical Digital File Management System (DMS) to various state government agencies. These agencies relied heavily on legacy paper-based workflows. Despite the mandate to digitize, the platform faced escalating churn and a dangerously low Customer Retention Rate (CRR) of just 15%. The product was technically robust but failing operationally because it was fundamentally misaligned with the actual capabilities and daily realities of its end-users.

MY ROLE

PM owning the DMS turnaround: field research across government offices, the simplification roadmap, and the tender-qualification pivot.

02. Strategy & Discovery

Bypassing Assumptions for Reality

Identified a severe misalignment between system buyers (senior administrative officials) and actual daily users (government clerks). Overhauling the discovery process prioritized direct field observations and shadowing clerks. Discovery metrics confirmed that the interface design was built for engineers, not administrative staff. Convoluted file indexing took longer digitally than legacy paper processing. This research prompted a structural product pivot, entirely freezing new feature development to focus on execution speed and interface simplification.

03. Feature Insight

Workflow Acceleration Metrics

By systematically pruning unnecessary fields, optimizing file categorization flows, and matching target technical benchmarks of state procurement specifications, the system achieved a massive turnaround in operational velocity.
