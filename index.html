Scaling Data-Driven Electricity Theft Detection

UPPCL • Product Consultant • 2024 - Present

Led the transformation of fragmented, manual field operations into a cohesive operational intelligence platform. Shipped an enforcement MVP to 8,000+ field officers across 5 districts in 6 months.

> Context

Massive revenue leak due to undetected electricity theft. Field officers operated blindly. Opportunity: A targeted $7M ARR uplift via optimized bill recovery workflows.

> Stakeholders

Primary: 8,000+ frontline enforcement officers (low tech-literacy).
Secondary: IAS-level government leadership, vendors.

> Strategy & Discovery

Conducted deep structured user discovery by shadowing frontline officers. Discovered the core issue wasn't just 'finding' theft, but proving it efficiently on-site to create an undeniable audit trail.
