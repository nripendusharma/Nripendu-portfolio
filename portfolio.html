Scaling Data-Driven Electricity Theft Detection

UPPCL • Product Consultant • 2024 - Present

Led the transformation of fragmented, manual field operations into a cohesive operational intelligence platform. Shipped an enforcement MVP to 8,000+ field officers across 5 districts in 6 months.

Context & Opportunity

Massive revenue leak due to undetected electricity theft. Field officers operated blindly without data context. The opportunity was a targeted $7M ARR uplift via optimized bill recovery workflows.

Users & Stakeholders

Primary: 8,000+ frontline enforcement officers (low tech-literacy).
Secondary: IAS-level government leadership, 12 engineers, vendor partners.

Product Strategy & Discovery

Conducted deep structured user discovery by shadowing frontline officers. Discovered that the core issue wasn't just 'finding' theft, but proving it efficiently on-site. Translated field observations into a prioritized feature spec.

MUST_HAVE: Geolocation tagging, offline-first syncing, consumption anomaly alerts.
SHOULD_HAVE: Automated report generation.
WON'T_HAVE (MVP): Predictive ML models (deferred to phase 2).

Enterprise Candidate Experience Redesign

Led an end-to-end redesign and legacy system migration of the Candidate App, deployed across 15+ countries for enterprise clients like Cambridge Assessments, Cisco, and Nvidia.

The Problem & Strategy

Candidate drop-off during online assessments was high due to legacy UX friction and system reliability issues. I instrumented score tracking to isolate the highest-friction moments across the journey.

Solution: Prioritized fixes using an Impact-to-Effort framework. Led the migration off legacy systems while simultaneously revamping the UX to prove that platform investments and customer outcomes are not a tradeoff.
